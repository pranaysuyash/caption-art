## SceneGuide: exhaustive feature list (no tech specs)

### 1) Onboarding, safety boundaries, and control

- First-run “what this can and cannot do” safety briefing (explicitly not a replacement for cane/dog, traffic rules, medical advice).
- Choose assistance style: “Low-vision scene narrator” vs “Elder safety helper” vs “Quiet mode” vs “High-alert mode.”
- Risk tolerance slider (calm, balanced, vigilant) affecting how often alerts interrupt.
- Preferred guidance style: short commands, step-by-step coaching, or descriptive narration.
- Language, speaking speed, voice tone, and verbosity controls.
- Accessibility-first setup: font size, contrast themes, haptic intensity, large targets.
- Consent prompts for camera/mic with plain-language explanations.
- Quick “panic quiet” button to stop speech immediately.
- “Hand-off mode” for caregiver: temporary settings adjustment without deep menus.

### 2) Core real-time scene understanding

- Instant scene summary (“where you are / what’s around you” in simple terms).
- Object detection with categories (people, vehicles, doors, stairs, furniture, pets, appliances, etc.).
- Spatial relationships: left/right/center, near/far, relative position and layout phrasing.
- Distance approximation cues and “approaching / receding” change detection.
- Orientation cues: what the camera is facing (pathway, wall, doorway, countertop, floor).
- “What changed since last moment” memory: new objects, moved objects, emerging hazards.
- “Focus guidance” to center the most relevant object in view (verbally and/or via haptics).
- Confidence-aware phrasing (hedged language when uncertain, without dumping numbers).
- Automatic de-duplication so it doesn’t repeat the same obvious facts every second.

### 3) Hazard detection and severity classification (what can hurt you, how bad, what to do)

- Trip hazards: cables, clutter, uneven surfaces, potholes, rugs, thresholds.
- Fall hazards: stairs, missing handrails, steep slopes, open manholes, wet floors.
- Collision hazards: poles, low-hanging signs, open cabinet doors, protruding objects.
- Burn hazards: hot utensils, stoves, kettles, irons, heaters, steaming vessels.
- Cuts/puncture hazards: broken glass, sharp tools, exposed nails, needles.
- Electrical hazards: exposed wires, wet electronics zones, power tool proximity.
- Traffic hazards: approaching vehicles, bikes, scooters, crossing risk cues.
- Crowd hazards: dense crowd ahead, fast movement, shoulder-to-shoulder congestion.
- Animal hazards: dogs nearby, sudden movement, leash entanglement risk cues.
- Environmental hazards: smoke/fire cues, poor lighting, glare, foggy/low visibility warnings.
- “Severity + rationale” output: brief reason + suggested action (“stop”, “step left”, “slow down”).
- “Compound hazard” detection: multiple small hazards elevating overall risk.
- False-alarm minimization modes (home vs street vs kitchen vs bathroom vs stairs).

### 4) Navigation-like guidance without being “navigation”

- “Safe-to-move” gating: clearly says when to stop vs proceed cautiously.
- Micro-guidance: step-left/step-right/forward/turn-slightly with minimal chatter.
- Path inference: identify open corridor vs blocked path vs obstacle field.
- Doorway guidance: locate door, handle position, whether door is open/closed.
- Stairs guidance: up/down direction, first step location, landing detection, edge awareness cues.
- Curb and ramp awareness: curb up/down, ramp slope warning, uneven pavement cues.
- “Find a seat” guidance: locate chair/sofa/bench, approach angle, obstacle-free path cues.
- “Find an exit” guidance: identify recognizable exit markers and open pathways.
- “Keep aligned” guidance: staying centered in hallway/walkway.

### 5) “Ask without typing”: intent-driven assistance

- One-tap “What’s in front of me?” quick query.
- “Describe the scene in more detail” on demand.
- “Where is the [object]?” query with guidance to locate it (keys, phone, bottle, remote).
- “Is it safe to…?” queries: “step forward”, “cross”, “sit”, “pick this up”, “pour”.
- “Read labels/signs” request when user wants precise text.
- “Identify this” for an item held close to camera (food, medicine box, tool, device).
- “Explain what to do next” for uncertain situations (e.g., “I’m at a door”, “I’m at stairs”).

### 6) Text and signage help (real-world utility)

- Read printed text: labels, menus, instructions, bills, receipts, packaging.
- Read signs: room numbers, restroom signs, street signs, shop boards.
- “Key details extraction” mode: price, size, expiry date, dosage instructions, warnings.
- “Summarize long text” into short, actionable bullets.
- Misread protection: “read twice” option that re-checks critical fields (expiry, dosage, amount).
- Multi-language reading and translation.
- Layout-aware reading: “top line”, “middle paragraph”, “bottom right price.”
- Sensitive text handling prompts (“this looks like a personal document, do you want me to read it aloud?”).

### 7) People and social context (helpful, not creepy)

- Count people nearby and their relative positions.
- “Approaching person” alert (someone moving toward you).
- Basic interaction cues: someone facing you, someone speaking (if enabled), group cluster nearby.
- Queue detection: “line seems to start to your right” style guidance.
- Social obstacle cueing: avoid bumping into people, gap detection in crowds.
- Optional “do not describe faces” privacy mode (only positions/avoidance).

### 8) Audio-aware fusion (situational awareness beyond vision)

- Detect important sound events and translate into plain-language alerts: horn, siren, alarm, dog bark, knocking.
- “Sound direction hinting” (left/right/behind) as best-effort cues.
- “Quiet environment” detection to reduce unnecessary alerts, “loud environment” to increase visual caution.

### 9) Domain-specific scenario packs (use-case driven)

**Home**

- Bathroom assistant: wet floor warnings, step/threshold detection, locating sink/toilet/shower.
- Living room: cable/clutter trip warnings, locating remote/phone/glasses, finding seating.
